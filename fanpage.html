Who is this guy, anyway?

Hwang In-youp broke into entertainment as a runway model in 2017, signing with KeyEast the following year almost by accident — he was there for a modeling look, and walked out with an acting career. His screen debut came via the 2018 web series WHY, and by 2019 he'd landed his first TV role as the loyal, sword-wielding sidekick in the historical drama The Tale of Nokdu.

Then 2020 happened twice: first as the lovable jock bully-turned-friend in the fantasy comedy 18 Again, and then as Han Seo-jun in True Beauty — the second lead so charismatic he basically hijacked the show's fandom. He even sang on the soundtrack. In 2022 he stepped into his first network-TV leading role with the mystery drama Why Her, proving the "second lead energy" could absolutely carry a whole series.

Off-screen, he grew up partly in Davao, the Philippines, where his classmates knew him as "Ryan Leon" — and he once dreamed of becoming a fashion designer before the entertainment industry got him first.
